# Where this data comes from — and the control you have over it

## The short version

Every emissions figure on this site was **published by the European
Commission and EMSA because the law requires it**. Article 21 of the EU MRV
Regulation ((EU) 2015/757) obliges the Commission to publish, for every ship
over 5,000 GT calling at an EEA port, its verified annual CO₂, fuel
consumption, efficiency and the company responsible. It is downloadable by
anyone, today, from `mrv.emsa.europa.eu`. No login. That is not a loophole —
public scrutiny is the purpose of the regulation.

So: **your competitor can already see your ship's verified emissions, with or
without us.** What they cannot easily do is understand what those numbers
cost you. That understanding is what we build — and that part, we control.

## What is public, what is ours

| | Source | Can it be hidden? |
|---|---|---|
| Verified CO₂, fuel, distance-intensity, technical efficiency (EEXI/EEDI/EIV), company name | **EMSA**, published under Art. 21 | No — it is public by law, one click away at the source |
| Cost exposure, CII rating, FuelEU balance, penalty risk, lever savings, fleet rankings | **CarbonKeel's own calculations** | **Yes — on the operator's request** |

Nobody can compel us to publish our own arithmetic about someone else's ship.
That is the control we can genuinely offer, and we offer it.

## Your controls

**1. Suppress our estimates.** If you operate a vessel and would rather our
derived figures were not shown to third parties, tell us and we switch them
off for that IMO — straight away, no negotiation, no "commercial discussion". We then
confirm by email once it has been made permanent.
Visitors then see the public EMSA facts plus a note that the operator opted
out. Use the **"Is this your ship?"** button on any vessel result.

**2. Claim your vessels.** Claiming (verified by company email or a Document
of Compliance) unlocks the full analysis for you and your colleagues, lets you
replace our estimates with your actual noon-report and bunker data, and marks
those figures as owner-verified rather than estimated.

**3. Correct the record.** Our public-data figures are reconstructions.
Distance is derived from the CO₂-per-mile you filed; the fuel mix is assumed;
deadweight is not published at all, so ratings depend on a capacity the
visitor types in. If a number is wrong, send the real one — we will use it and
label it as yours.

**4. Alternative publication (Art. 21(6)).** Where EMSA has accepted an
operator's request to publish certain fields differently, that is reflected in
the export we download. We refresh from source so those decisions carry
through to us.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell or share the lookup activity of our visitors.
- We do not publish a downloadable copy of the register, or offer bulk export
  of derived figures to anonymous users. Fleet-scale analysis is rate-limited
  and capped precisely because harvesting — not the single honest lookup — is
  the behaviour that would harm operators.
- We do not let search engines index per-vessel pages. Searching a ship's name
  on Google will not surface our estimate of its compliance bill.
- We never present a derived estimate as an official or verified figure.
  Everything reconstructed carries an **ESTIMATE** label, and the method is
  published in full so any verifier can check it.

## Why we show anything at all

Because the alternative is worse for you. Compliance exposure is already being
computed about your fleet — by charterers screening tonnage, by banks scoring
Poseidon Principles alignment, by cargo owners under the Sea Cargo Charter,
and by your competitors' analysts. Those calculations happen without you, using
the same public data, with no label saying "estimate" and no way for you to
correct them.

We would rather the number in circulation be one you have seen, can audit
line-by-line, and can replace with your own verified data.
